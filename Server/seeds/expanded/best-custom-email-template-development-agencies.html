How to Choose the Right Email Template Development Agency

  1. Match the provider to your platform. A Klaviyo-heavy store needs Klaviyo experience; a B2B team on HubSpot needs HubL module skills.
  2. Ask how they test. Look for testing in Outlook, Gmail, Apple Mail, mobile apps and dark mode.
  3. Check who writes the code. Direct access to the developer usually means fewer mistakes and faster fixes.
  4. Compare total cost, not just price. Include revisions, testing and platform setup.
  5. Review real work. Ask for examples in your industry, like the MailStora portfolio.

Questions to ask before you hire

  • Is the code hand-written, or exported from a builder? Can I see a sample of the raw HTML?
  • Which email clients and devices do you test in, and do I get screenshots?
  • How do you handle classic Outlook for Windows? Do you use VML for buttons and background images?
  • Will the template be editable in my ESP's visual editor, or only in code?
  • How many revision rounds are included, and what counts as a revision?
  • Who owns the code once it is paid for?
  • What happens if a rendering bug shows up after delivery?

Red flags to watch for

  • No testing evidence. If they cannot show client screenshots, assume the email was only checked in a browser.
  • Div-based or framework code. Web page code that looks fine in Gmail often breaks in Outlook.
  • Everything as one big image. Image-only emails hurt accessibility, get clipped by image blocking and can hurt deliverability.
  • Unclear pricing. If you cannot tell what you will pay before starting, expect surprises.
  • No ESP setup knowledge. A template that works as a file but breaks when uploaded to Klaviyo or Mailchimp is not finished.
